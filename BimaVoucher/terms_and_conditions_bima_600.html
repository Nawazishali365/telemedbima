BCare Lite – Terms & Conditions

OVERVIEW

BCare Lite is a healthcare service offered by MILVIK Mobile Pakistan (Private) Limited ("BIMA", "MILVIK", "we", "our", or "us"). The Service provides Subscribers with access to 24/7 telemedicine consultations, specialist consultations and referrals, diagnostic support, medicine support, and other healthcare services through BIMA's approved healthcare network.

All healthcare services under BCare Lite begin with a consultation with a BIMA Doctor, who will assess the Subscriber's condition and determine the appropriate clinical pathway and eligible benefits.

FEATURES AND BENEFITS:

The following benefits are available during an active subscription, subject to the applicable annual and monthly limits.

A. 24/7 Tele-Doctor

  • Unlimited consultations with BIMA doctors via dedicated helpline 042-111 11 2462.
  • Access to specialist consultations through appointment scheduling where medically appropriate.

B. Physical Clinic (External GP & Specialist Consultation Support)

  • Available only after assessment and referral by a BIMA Doctor.
  • Annual support limit: PKR 6,000.
  • Monthly support limit: PKR 1,000.

C. Diagnostic Tests

  • Available only when prescribed by a BIMA Doctor.
  • Services must be obtained through BIMA's approved laboratory partners.
  • Annual support limit: PKR 6,000.
  • Monthly support limit: PKR 1,000.

D. Medicines

  • Available only for medicines prescribed by a BIMA Doctor or through an approved referral pathway.
  • Medicines must be obtained through BIMA's approved pharmacy network.
  • Annual support limit: PKR 3,000.
  • Monthly support limit: PKR 500.

CLINICAL PATHWAY:

BCare Lite follows a managed healthcare model. Every healthcare event must begin with a consultation with a BIMA Doctor. Following clinical assessment, the BIMA Doctor may recommend:

  • Health advice only;
  • Medicines;
  • Diagnostic tests;
  • Referral to a General Practitioner;
  • Referral to an approved specialist.

Benefits are available only when services are obtained through the pathway determined by the BIMA Doctor.

APPROVED HEALTHCARE NETWORK:

Benefits are available only through healthcare providers, laboratories, pharmacies and specialists approved by BIMA.

Services obtained outside the approved network are not eligible unless specifically approved by BIMA.

BENEFIT LIMITS:

  • Monthly limits cannot be carried forward.
  • Annual limits apply across the subscription year.

SERVICES NOT COVERED:

The following are not eligible for benefits under BCare Lite:

  • Walk-in consultations without referral from a BIMA Doctor.
  • Self-medication.
  • Medicines purchased without a valid prescription issued through the approved pathway.
  • Laboratory test not prescribed by a BIMA Doctor.
  • Cosmetic or aesthetic procedures.
  • Experimental treatments.
  • Wellness products, vitamins and food supplements unless specifically approved within the treatment pathway.
  • Emergency medical treatment.
  • Hospital admission and inpatient treatment.
  • Ambulance services.
  • Medical services obtained outside Pakistan.
  • Any service not specifically included within the subscription benefits.

ELIGIBILITY:

  • The Subscription is available to eligible customers aged 18 years and above.
  • Subscription benefits are available only while the Subscription remains active and the applicable subscription charges are successfully collected.
  • The Subscription is non-transferable and may only be used by the registered Subscriber.

SERVICE SUBSCRIPTION PERIOD:

  • Service remains active for as long as the subscription is successfully renewed.
  • Benefits become unavailable if the subscription expires or cannot be renewed.
  • BIMA reserves the right to suspend or terminate subscription in cases of fraud, misuse or violation of these Terms.
